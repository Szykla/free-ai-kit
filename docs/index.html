The kit

Ten skills, ready to copy.

Each is one plain-text file in the open Agent Skills format. Drop it into Claude Code, a Claude or ChatGPT project, or paste it into any chat.

chief-of-staff

A short, numbered brief of what needs you today. Answer "do 2 and 4".

advisor

Stress-test a big decision with four opposing views and one clear recommendation.

find-work-to-automate

Rank your recurring work by how much AI can take off your plate.

skill-builder

Turn a job you just did with AI into a tested, reusable skill.

no-slop

Write so it doesn't read like AI wrote it.

grill-me

Get interviewed until every decision in your plan is settled.

retro

After a mistake, fix the setup so it doesn't happen again.

handoff

A note that lets a fresh chat pick up your work cold.

debug

Find the real cause of a broken script before changing anything.

weekly-review

What moved, what's stuck, what to drop, on one screen.
