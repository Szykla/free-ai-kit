## Production notes

- One task per video: one sort, one test, one plan line.
- Film the paper parts from above with good light; keep handwriting large. Use the kit's three column colours: cyan for "AI does it", violet for "AI helps", pink for "I do it".
- Record the chat parts in a clean demo account with no notifications. Use a modest window size with larger zoom.
- Use a real human voice at a conversational pace, and hold the marked pauses.
- Show the study source on screen while row 3 plays. Say "about" for every figure, as the script does.
- Label every AI reply "Example: yours will differ".
- Keep on-screen text to short labels; do not repeat the narration word for word.
- Upload hand-edited captions made from the Audio column, and publish the same text as the transcript next to the written lesson.
- Re-record if the lesson's study figures or sources change. Last checked: November 2026.
