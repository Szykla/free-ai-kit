## Production notes

- Record in a clean demo Google account with a few fake supplier emails. Never show a real inbox. Use solid boxes, not blur, over any address.
- The fire-drill email uses the reserved address test@example.com, which cannot receive mail. Do not change it to a real address.
- Before recording, recheck **Settings > Plugins > Permissions**. OpenAI notes the menu may say **Apps** instead of **Plugins** on some accounts. If it differs, update the script and the lesson together.
- Use a modest window at 125% zoom and the kit's one pink highlight style. Record a human voice, with 2 to 5 second pauses after each click.
- Upload captions from the Audio column, and publish this script as the transcript beside the written lesson.
- The narration names every on-screen action, so it also covers audio description.
