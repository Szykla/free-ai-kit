Order a celebration cake

Birthdays, weddings, a Tuesday that needs cheering up.

  1. Choose a size. 6 inch serves 8 people. 8 inch serves 14.
  2. Pick a flavour. Vanilla, chocolate or lemon.
  3. Call or email us at least three days before you need it.
  4. Collect it from the shop. You pay when you collect.

Allergies: everything is made in one small kitchen that uses wheat, milk, eggs, nuts and sesame. Please tell us about allergies when you order, and we will talk it through with you.

Prices: 6 inch from £28, 8 inch from £40. We confirm the price by phone or email before we bake.
