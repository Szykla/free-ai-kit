# The tutorials

Sixteen free lessons, from your first useful chat to publishing an app. Each one follows Maria, who runs a small bakery, then asks you to try it on your own life. Every lesson has a word-for-word video script; the videos come later.

Checked: November 2026. AI apps change fast. If a button has moved, the lesson names it in words so you can find it.

## How these were made

We read the free courses from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Hugging Face and DeepLearning.AI, plus the best independent teachers, then wrote our own lessons. Every lesson lists its sources at the bottom. Facts, prices and plan limits were checked on the official pages in November 2026. Where two official pages disagree, the lesson says so.
