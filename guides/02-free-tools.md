# Free tools instead of "$600 in discounts"

Paid bundles list trial discounts on tools that you then pay for. This list is the
other way round: tools that are free, open-source, or included in a plan you may
already have. Limits change often; check each tool's own page before relying on one.

Last checked: November 2026.

## Thinking and writing
- **Free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.** Enough for daily drafting.
- **Google AI Studio.** Free Gemini API access with rate limits, good for experiments.
- **OpenRouter models tagged `:free`.** Many open models at zero cost, rate-limited.
- **Ollama / LM Studio.** Run open models on your own machine.

## Voice (instead of paid dictation apps)
- **Your phone's and OS's built-in dictation.** Free and usually good enough.
- **Whisper / faster-whisper, Parakeet.** Free local speech-to-text.
- **Kokoro, Piper.** Free local text-to-speech.

## Notes, meetings and knowledge
- **Obsidian** or any folder of Markdown files: the best "second brain" is text files
  the AI can read.
- **MarkItDown** (Microsoft, MIT): turns PDFs, Word, Excel and slides into Markdown an
  AI reads well.
- Meeting notes: record (with consent), transcribe locally with Whisper, then ask
  your AI for decisions and action items.

## Building apps and sites
- **Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI.** Agents that write and run code; they use
  your existing plan or a free tier.
- **GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages.** Free static hosting with HTTPS.
- **VS Code.** Free editor.

## Automation
- **Your AI tool's own scheduled tasks** (where offered) for a daily brief.
- **Windows Task Scheduler / cron.** Free, already on your machine, run a script on
  a schedule.
- **Playwright.** Free browser automation, for a website job you do every week.

## Checking AI work
- **promptfoo** (MIT): test a prompt or skill against cases before you trust it.
- A second model as reviewer: paste the output into a different AI and ask what's wrong.

## What's honestly worth paying for
- One good plan (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) if you use AI daily. One, not three.
- Nothing else until a free tool has measurably held you back.
