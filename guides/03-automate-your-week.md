# Automate your week

## 1. Find the work
Run the `find-work-to-automate` skill, or answer these yourself: what did you do more
than twice last week that followed the same steps? Which of those steps are reading,
sorting, summarizing or drafting? Those are AI's. Steps that are deciding, sending or
paying stay yours.

## 2. Climb one rung at a time
1. **A prompt you type.** Do the job with AI once, by hand, and correct it as you go.
2. **A skill.** Turn that conversation into a skill with `skill-builder`. Your
   corrections become its rules.
3. **A schedule.** Once the skill ran clean three times in a row without you fixing
   anything, schedule it (daily brief, weekly review).
4. **A report you only read.** The schedule writes to a file or a note; you look at it.

A run that needs fixing drops it one rung. The approval steps never go away: a
schedule never makes sending or spending automatic.

## 3. Keep it honest
- Every time you correct the AI, run `retro` and put the fix into the skill, not into
  your memory.
- Once a month, look at your schedules: drop the ones you stopped reading.
