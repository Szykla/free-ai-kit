# The best free AI courses (and what to skip)

The companies that build the models now teach them for free, and their courses are
better and newer than most paid ones. Every link here was checked on its own page on
**10 November 2026**. Prices and content change, so look again before you rely on one.

## If you're new: do these three, in this order (about 8 hours)

| Course | From | Time | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Foundations](https://academy.openai.com/) | OpenAI Academy | 60-75 min | Prompts, context and checking output, with ChatGPT. Free with any ChatGPT account. |
| [AI Fluency: Framework and foundations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations) | Claude Academy (Anthropic) | 4 hr | How to split work between you and AI, and when not to trust it. Works with any tool. |
| [AI capabilities and limitations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-capabilities-and-limitations) | Claude Academy | 3.5 hr | An honest mental model of what these models can and can't do. Saves you from most beginner mistakes. |

## If you want AI to do real work for you

| Course | From | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Applied AI Foundations](https://academy.openai.com/) | OpenAI Academy | 75-90 min | Turns one task you do every week into a workflow. |
| [Agents and Workflows](https://academy.openai.com/public/courses/agents-and-workflows-bieml) | OpenAI Academy | ~90 min | Handing tasks to agents with boundaries and checkpoints. Needs a plan with agent access. |
| [Introduction to agent skills](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-agent-skills) | Claude Academy | 1 hr | Skills like the ones in this kit: how to write, share and fix them. |
| [Claude Code 101](https://academy.claude.com/products/code/courses) and [Claude Code in action](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-code-in-action) | Claude Academy | 1.5 hr + 1 hr | Coding agents, from first run to long runs you can trust. Claude Code needs a paid Claude plan. |

## If you want to build agents yourself (some Python helps)

| Resource | From | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) | Anthropic | Short essay, no code. Read it first: most "agents" should be simple workflows. |
| [A practical guide to building agents](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/) | OpenAI | 34-page PDF, no code. |
| [AI Agents Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course) | Hugging Face | Free, with a free certificate. Needs basic Python. Apache-2.0 on GitHub. |
| [5-Day AI Agents Intensive](https://www.kaggle.com/learn-guide/5-day-agents) | Google + Kaggle | Self-paced: whitepapers and codelabs. Needs a free AI Studio key. |
| [AI Agents for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners) | Microsoft | Lessons on GitHub, MIT. Readable without running the code. |
| [Introduction to Model Context Protocol](https://academy.claude.com/products/code/courses) | Claude Academy | Connect AI to your own tools and data. |
| [Introduction to evals](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-evals) | Claude Academy | How to know whether your AI setup actually works. |
| [Prompt engineering interactive tutorial](https://github.com/anthropics/courses/tree/master/prompt_engineering_interactive_tutorial) | Anthropic | 9 chapters with exercises. Older models, same lessons. |

## Skip or be careful
- **"Free" courses with a paid certificate.** The videos may be free and the badge not.
  Google AI Essentials is often listed as free but runs on a Coursera subscription in
  many countries.
- **"Make money with AI" groups.** Most AI groups on Skool's discovery page (checked
  10 Nov 2026) sell an income promise for $9 to $329 a month: AI agencies, AI video
  ads, selling automations to businesses. The AI skills underneath are in the courses
  above. Pay for a group only if you want the people (feedback, calls, accountability),
  never for the lessons.
- **Certificates as proof.** Nobody hires for a badge. Something you built and can show
  counts for more.
