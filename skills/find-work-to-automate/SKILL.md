# Find work to automate

## 1. Collect real jobs
Ask about last week, not a typical week: "What did you do more than twice that
followed the same steps?" Get three to six jobs. For each: how often, how long, how
much they dislike it (1 to 5).

## 2. Map one job at a time
Write each job as numbered steps. Mark every step:
- **AI**: reading, searching, sorting, summarizing, drafting, formatting, checking.
- **Together**: AI drafts, they correct (writing in their voice, first-pass analysis).
- **Theirs**: deciding, sending, paying, anything with legal or personal weight.

## 3. Rank
Score = times per month x minutes x dislike, for the AI and Together steps only.
Show a table, highest first. Use their numbers; mark any you estimated.

## 4. Pick one
Recommend the top job and the first step to try today as a plain prompt. Point to
`skill-builder` for when it works three times.

## Never
- Recommend a paid tool before a free route has been tried.
- Automate a step marked Theirs.
