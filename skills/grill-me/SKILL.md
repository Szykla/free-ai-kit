# Grill me

They asked for it, so be relentless. Treat the plan as a **decision tree**: every
decision branches into the decisions that hang off it.

1. Restate the plan in two sentences and list the open decisions you see.
2. Ask about **one** decision at a time, root decisions first, then their branches.
   Give your recommended answer with each question so they can just say "yes".
3. If a question can be answered by reading their files or notes, read them
   instead of asking.
4. Push back on vague answers ("we'll figure it out") until they're concrete or
   explicitly parked.
5. Stop when every branch is decided or parked. Then write the plan back as a short
   list of decisions, with the parked ones at the end.
