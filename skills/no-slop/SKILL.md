# No slop

Readers spot AI text by its habits, not its ideas. Remove the habits.

## Cut on sight
- Openers and closers: "In today's fast-paced world", "Great question", "In
  conclusion", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if...".
- Inflated words: delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, leverage, unlock,
  elevate, seamless, robust, game-changer, navigate (for anything but maps).
- Hedges stacked on hedges: "it's important to note that it may potentially".
- The "not X, but Y" flourish and "It's not just A, it's B" more than once a page.
- Lists of three by reflex. Use two or four if that's what's true.
- Em-dashes as the default punctuation. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Bold on every other phrase. Bold the one thing they must not miss, or nothing.
- Emoji as bullet points, unless the platform's natives write that way.

## Do instead
- Lead with the point. The first sentence should be the one they'd remember.
- Concrete beats abstract: a number, a name, an example.
- Short sentences, plain words, active verbs. Vary sentence length.
- Write like the person, not like a brochure. If you have samples of their writing,
  match their sentence length and favourite words.

## Check before handing over
Read it aloud in your head. Any sentence a human wouldn't say to a friend gets
rewritten. Then count: any word from the "cut" list left? Fix and recount.
