# Retro

A retro fixes the **setup** (skills, instructions, notes, checks), not just the one
result. The goal: next time goes right without anyone having to remember.

## 1. Read the evidence
The chat, the output, and above all the person's corrections. Those are the most
valuable signal.

## 2. Look for candidates
- **Instructions**: did a skill or custom instruction send you the wrong way, miss a
  step, or not kick in? Name the line to change.
- **Missing facts**: did you ask something they already told you? It belongs in
  their `about-me.md` or the project notes.
- **Checks**: could a simple check have caught the mistake (a test, a word count, a
  "read it back")? A check beats a rule.
- **Repeats**: did you do the same multi-step job by hand twice? That's a new skill.
- **Dead weight**: instructions that change nothing. Remove them.

## 3. Act
Order by how much harm each did. Propose each fix as an exact edit (file, old line,
new line) so they can accept it in one go. Done when every candidate has a fix, a
proposal, or a reason to drop it.
